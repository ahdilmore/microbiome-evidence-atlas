UFPF / SID COMPATIBILITY AUDIT
Audit date: 2026-10-17

VERDICT
Suitable candidate for an exploratory, disease-labeled cross-sectional comparison after explicit normalization and metadata handling. Not certified as an identical end-to-end pipeline, and not a longitudinal reference or diagnostic classifier. Include PD, IBD and control samples; keep the collection context visible.

DATABASE AND SOFTWARE
Both UFPF TSV headers and its processing commands explicitly name mpa_vJun23_CHOCOPhlAnSGB_202307, matching Sid's lab report. Sid reports MetaPhlAn 4.1.1. UFPF documentation says MetaPhlAn4 (README describes 4.0); exact patch version is not established by merged headers/resource table. Do not claim an exact software-version match.
UFPF documents BBDuk Q25 trimming, minimum 50 bases, adapter/PhiX filtering, GRCh38 human-read removal, low-complexity filtering, and consistent profiling settings across two sequencing batches. The supplied script is documentation, not sample-level execution logs. Sid's full preprocessing/collection protocol is not documented in the supplied report, so equivalence remains unverified.
UFPF used stool swabs, PowerSoil Pro extraction, Nextera XT libraries and NovaSeq 2x150 sequencing targeting 3 million reads. Post-QC read depth varies widely (139,618 to 10,077,039 across archived metadata); non-detection, richness and low-abundance taxa need special caution. Sid's supplied integer weights must not be treated as equivalent read counts.

ABUNDANCE AND TAXONOMY
No duplicate taxon rows/sample columns, negative abundances, or nonfinite values were found in the GitHub tables. Leaf-level totals are 99.99995-100.00006%, consistent with rounding.
The table contains all taxonomic ranks: never sum parents and children together.
Two available versions differ by whether estimated unclassified organisms are included. UNCLASSIFIED spans 0-40.62946%. Renormalizing the classified rows of that version recovers the other table to within 0.000009 percentage points.
Three eukaryotic leaf taxa are present. SGB-only sums can be as low as 98.61391%; Sid's app normalizes within supplied SGB weights. Any comparison must explicitly use the same scope/denominator and retain excluded-mass information.
There are 23 UFPF '_group' SGB rows. Do not strip that suffix and assume equivalence to a numeric Sid ID. Three numeric Sid IDs have such potentially ambiguous counterparts (1990, 4425, 16971).
Among taxa actually detected in the 96 archived samples, 176 strictly matching ungrouped SGB IDs overlap Sid's 236. They account for 95.97%, 88.41%, 93.04%, 92.28%, and 93.93% of Sid's reported abundance, in chronological order. This measures observed overlap, not database coverage or assay sensitivity. Missing taxa are not proven absent. Use a union of supported identifiers for a future community comparison, with uncertainty flags; do not silently drop all non-overlapping taxa and renormalize the intersection.

COLLECTION CONTEXT
The UFPF IBD participants were in remission. They collected the first bowel movement after starting polyethylene-glycol colonoscopy preparation. Differences may reflect bowel prep as well as disease/medication. Retain this group with an explicit bowel-prep label and show analyses with and without it.
Controls were spouses (mostly of PD participants), not a random population sample. Shared households and age distributions matter. Similar processing alone does not establish an unbiased health reference.

RECOMMENDED NEXT IMPLEMENTATION
Use the 96-sample archive as the reference source; preserve one diagnosis-ambiguous record. Offer all samples, PD, IBD-with-bowel-prep, and control groups separately. Start with abundant taxa/SGBs and relative composition, with explicit denominator and grouped-SGB handling. Show Sid's five observations against distributions, without disease predictions, p-values treating his visits as independent people, or a normal-trajectory score. Confirm Sid preprocessing and abundance-output semantics before stronger claims. Do not pool the separate Wallen/HMP2 tables in this workbook automatically: they are not demonstrated here to share the UFPF pipeline.

No website changes, read reprocessing, or clinical classification were performed.
